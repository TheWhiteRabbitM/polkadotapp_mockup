Try the Polkadot super-app. Right now.

A working replica of the open-source iOS Host (polkadot-ios-community): pick a username, chat, switch the 5 themes, run the W3S Pay merchant terminal, send CASH — while real blocks, assets and identities stream in from Polkadot's Paseo testnets.

⚠️ NOTHING IS STORED. ANYWHERE.

No accounts. No servers. No cookies. No localStorage. No analytics.
Everything you type — including your username — lives only in this browser tab's memory and vanishes the moment you refresh. Payments and balances are simulated; chain data is read-only from public testnets. This is an unofficial demo, unaffiliated with Parity. Not financial advice.
