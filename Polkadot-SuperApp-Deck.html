thesis

A native HOST (the Polkadot App) runs sandboxed mini-apps called PRODUCTS, which talk to the device and the chain through an OS-grade API: TrUAPI.

"Tru" = Triangle

Triangle User-Agent Programming Interface. Three corners, minimised trust:

  • Host mediates — holds keys, grants capabilities.
  • Product is sandboxed — gets only what the user allows.
  • Chain settles — Asset Hub, Bulletin, Statement Store.

append-only wire IDs → products never break across protocol upgrades.

identity // scoped by design

One you,
many faces.

You sign in once with a .dot identity (DotNS). But every product gets its own derived account — it never sees your global wallet.

// per-product account
accountGet({ productAccountId:{
  dotNsIdentifier: "my-app.dot",
  derivationIndex: 0 }})

Why it matters

A merchant app sees a scoped account, not your identity graph. Capability-security applied to apps — sandboxing the financial self.

  • host_get_user_id — JIT-approved primary identity
  • host_account_get — derived, per-product
  • legacy_accounts — bring imported keys

privacy // on-device crypto

Encrypt
without asking
the chain.

host_derive_entropy mints 32 bytes of deterministic entropy, scoped to the product + a key, via 3-layer BLAKE2b — feeding X25519 keys for peer-to-peer end-to-end encryption. No round-trips.

Privacy stack

  • Per-product accounts — isolation
  • JIT permissions — least privilege
  • derive_entropy → X25519 — E2E
  • Self-custodial — keys stay with you

privacy by architecture, not by promise.

analyst caveats // what to watch

Read the fine print.

Where's the Host?

No public polkadot-host repo found. OSS = protocol + SDK + products. The app-store policy is where "permissionless" gets tested.

Pre-product

Everything is Paseo testnet + "not audited". Shape & timeline can still move.

Balance custody

What backs top_up? DOT, stablecoin, or Host IOU? Off-ramp & abuse limits unspecified.

Regulation

NFC tap-to-pay at scale meets KYC/AML. Absent from the OSS so far.
