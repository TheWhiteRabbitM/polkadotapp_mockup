"Tru" = Triangle

Triangle User-Agent Programming Interface. Three corners, minimised trust:

  • Host mediates — holds keys, grants capabilities.
  • Product is sandboxed — gets only what the user allows.
  • Chain settles — Asset Hub, Bulletin, People.

append-only wire IDs → deployed products never break across protocol upgrades. There's even a Triangle-js SDK on the map.

Why it matters

A merchant app sees a scoped account, not your identity graph. Capability-security applied to apps — sandboxing the financial self.

  • JIT permissions — least privilege, "Allow always / once / never"
  • derive_entropy → X25519 — E2E without round-trips
  • Self-custodial — keys stay with you

privacy // on-device crypto

Encrypt
without asking
the chain.

host_derive_entropy mints 32 bytes of deterministic entropy, scoped to the product + a key, via 3-layer BLAKE2b — feeding X25519 keys for peer-to-peer end-to-end encryption. No round-trips.

Privacy stack

  • Per-product accounts — isolation
  • JIT permissions — least privilege
  • derive_entropy → X25519 — E2E
  • PoP without doxxing — verified-human tier, not KYC dossiers

privacy by architecture, not by promise.

analyst caveats // what to watch

Read the fine print.

Update: the iOS Host is OPEN

polkadot-ios-community shipped (GPL-3, Swift, 28 pkgs): chat+calls E2E, username payments w/ pool auto-swap, sandboxed dApps, PoP via DIM2 gesture calls. Still 404: Android, Desktop, W3S Pay.

Pre-product

Everything public is Paseo testnet + "not audited". Shape & timeline can still move.

Balance custody

What backs top_up? DOT, stablecoin, or Host IOU? Off-ramp & abuse limits unspecified. W3S Pay code still closed.

PoP & regulation

Proof-of-personhood tiers are elegant, but tap-to-pay at scale still meets KYC/AML. Absent from the OSS so far.
