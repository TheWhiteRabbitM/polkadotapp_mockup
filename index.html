web3 summit · live demo, recreated for everyone

The Polkadot super-app.
Now everyone can try it.

Today it was unveiled with a live demo at the Web3 Summit — on stage, for the people in the room. So I rebuilt it for everyone else: a hands-on, as-close-to-real-as-possible recreation, pieced together from Parity's open-source repos and the public app build.

Pick a username, check in, chat & call, pay at the merchant terminal, scan a QR, play the gesture game, earn your membership — the whole experience, right in your browser. Try all 5 themes too.

⚠️ A recreation — not the real app

This is an independent, community recreation for people who weren't at the Web3 Summit demo — not the official app. It runs fully offline: no blockchain, no network, no wallet, no accounts, no servers, no cookies, no analytics.
Every balance, payment, identity and check-in is simulated, stored nowhere, and gone the moment you refresh. Unaffiliated with Parity / Polkadot. No real value. Not financial advice.
